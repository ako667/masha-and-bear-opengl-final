# AI Use

OpenAI Codex assisted with the C++ implementation, GLSL shaders, procedural models and material patterns, build configuration, documentation, report and presentation. The procedural scene assets are generated in code. The report uses actual application captures and recorded benchmark results. It does not invent earlier milestone submissions, student authorship contributions or user-study results.

The students must review the code, verify its behaviour and explain its techniques. Any final submission must follow the instructor's AI policy. The character names and visual inspiration refer to Masha and the Bear, whose rights belong to the respective holders including Animaccord. The executable uses original stylised geometry and contains no extracted cartoon models, textures or shaders.
