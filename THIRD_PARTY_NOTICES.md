# Third party notices

The dependency source trees retain their original notices. No library licence is replaced by this summary.

- GLFW 3.4, https://github.com/glfw/glfw/tree/3.4. zlib/libpng licence in `third_party/glfw/LICENSE.md`.
- GLM 1.0.1, https://github.com/g-truc/glm/tree/1.0.1. MIT/Happy Bunny licence choices in `third_party/glm/copying.txt`.
- GLEW 2.2.0 with the Perlmint CMake wrapper, https://github.com/Perlmint/glew-cmake/tree/glew-cmake-2.2.0. GLEW, Mesa and Khronos notices are retained at the beginning of `third_party/glew/include/GL/glew.h` and related sources. The wrapper is an unofficial CMake packaging of GLEW, not an official new renderer.
- stb_easy_font by Sean Barrett and contributors, https://github.com/nothings/stb. Public-domain/MIT alternatives in `third_party/stb_easy_font.h`. The exact retrieved file is vendored for reproducibility.

The source repository excludes the user-supplied cartoon screenshots and character reference. They did not include redistribution licence information. The presentation identifies any supplied reference as reference imagery, separate from actual screenshots of the application. Attribution does not create a commercial licence or imply affiliation.

No third-party 3D models, raster material textures or shader programs are loaded by the executable. Generated geometry, shader patterns and project documentation are disclosed in `AI_USE.md`.
